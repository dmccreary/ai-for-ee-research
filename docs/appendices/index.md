# List of Appendices

The appendices hold supporting research that is not yet part of a chapter.
They are working notes and will be revised as the book develops.

1. [Personal Agents](personal-agents/index.md) - A sample team of seven agent
   personas for a researcher's dashboard, each with a character sheet and image
   prompt, followed by research notes, gathered in October 2026, on always-on
   personal AI agents: SpaceXAI's Grok Bot, OpenAI's Dots, Meta's Muse,
   Anthropic's Claude Cowork, and the open-source OpenClaw and Hermes Agent.
   The research compares their architecture, permission models, multi-agent
   features, pricing, and open questions, and notes that most claims are
   vendor-stated with no independent testing.
2. [Personal Knowledge Graphs](pkg/index.md) - A summary of Dan McCreary's prior
   research on personal knowledge graphs (PKGs) and how to integrate them
   securely into an organization's collective knowledge. It covers where PKGs
   came from, why people use them, the tension between free-form notes and
   structured knowledge, steps for enterprise integration, and role-based
   access control.
