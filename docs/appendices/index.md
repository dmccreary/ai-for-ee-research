# List of Appendices

The appendices hold supporting research that is not yet part of a chapter.
They are working notes and will be revised as the book develops.

1. [Agent Personas](agent-personas/index.md) - A sample team of seven agent
   personas for a researcher's dashboard (Ada, Scout, Marisol, Penny, Rosa,
   Pixel, and Kai), each with a character sheet, approval rules, and an image
   prompt.
2. [Personal Agent Software](personal-agent-software/index.md) - Research
   notes, gathered in October 2026, on always-on personal AI agent software:
   SpaceXAI's Grok Bot, OpenAI's Dots, Meta's Muse, Anthropic's Claude Cowork,
   and the open-source OpenClaw and Hermes Agent. The research compares their
   architecture, permission models, multi-agent features, pricing, and open
   questions, and notes that most claims are vendor-stated with no independent
   testing.
3. [Agent Email](agent-email.md) - Research notes, gathered in October 2026, on
   whether Grok Bot, Dots, Muse, Claude Cowork, and Gemini Spark can have their
   own email address or text messaging channel, and which third-party inbox and
   phone-number services can add one.
4. [Personal Knowledge Graphs](pkg/index.md) - A summary of Dan McCreary's prior
   research on personal knowledge graphs (PKGs) and how to integrate them
   securely into an organization's collective knowledge. It covers where PKGs
   came from, why people use them, the tension between free-form notes and
   structured knowledge, steps for enterprise integration, and role-based
   access control.
