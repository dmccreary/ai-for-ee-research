# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook. This
image will be used on the book's home page and in social media previews.

## Required Specifications

- Format: PNG
- Size: 1200 × 630 pixels (1.91:1 Open Graph aspect ratio)
- Wide landscape composition that remains legible as a small preview
- No watermark, logos, interface chrome, or stray text

The image has four visual layers, back to front: technical background,
integrated concept montage, mascot, and title.

## Subject and Tone

**AI for EE Research** is a hands-on textbook for graduate students,
postdoctoral researchers, and faculty in electrical and computer engineering.
It teaches researchers to orchestrate teams of AI planning and execution
agents for literature search, scientific coding, simulation, data analysis,
manuscript preparation, and reproducible research. The researcher remains
responsible for direction, domain judgment, verification, and the final
scientific claims.

The visual tone should be modern, precise, energetic, and technically
credible—optimistic about AI assistance without implying that automation
replaces scientific judgment.

## Title

Render the exact title **“AI for EE Research”** once, centered in a clean,
large, highly legible geometric sans-serif typeface. Spell it exactly. Use
white or very pale lettering with a subtle navy scrim or shadow. Preserve a
generous quiet area behind the title so it remains readable over the montage.
Do not add a subtitle or any other words.

## Integrated Concept Montage

Arrange these nine visual motifs as a coherent technical illustration around
the title—not as disconnected rectangular screenshots:

1. **Researcher as orchestrator** — a graduate EE researcher at a central
   workstation, directing a branching constellation of small planning and
   execution agent nodes while retaining a bright human-review checkpoint.
2. **Research workflow** — a subtle left-to-right pipeline from papers and
   citations to hypothesis, experiment or simulation, analysis, and a finished
   manuscript.
3. **Agent loop** — a compact glowing cycle linking a language model,
   tools, memory, action, observation, and human review.
4. **Electrical-engineering research** — an elegant circuit trace, oscilloscope
   waveform, and semiconductor or mixed-signal schematic motif.
5. **Scientific coding and data analysis** — restrained code-like glyphs,
   a notebook panel, and a clean plotted signal with uncertainty bands.
6. **Literature search and citation verification** — connected paper cards,
   magnifying glass, DOI-like link nodes, and a prominent verification check.
7. **Personal knowledge graph** — linked nodes representing papers,
   experiments, people, datasets, and projects.
8. **Reproducibility and trust** — a provenance trail with review gates,
   test marks, and a shield/check symbol; human approval remains visible.
9. **Interactive MicroSims** — a small abstract control panel with sliders,
   nodes, and a scientific plot, integrated into the technical scene.

## Mascot

Place **Volt**, the book's black-and-white border collie mascot, in the
lower-left corner, occupying roughly 18–22% of the image height. Match the
provided reference image: friendly amber eyes, one ear raised and one ear
slightly flopped, maroon bandana with a gold lightning-bolt emblem, and a
small gold whistle. Volt should look curious and welcoming and should not
overlap the title. Preserve the character's identity and colors accurately.

## Style and Composition

- Style: polished editorial vector illustration with subtle isometric depth,
  crisp linework, clean scientific UI motifs, and restrained glow effects.
- Palette: deep navy and electric blue, with teal and warm amber highlights;
  use maroon sparingly to connect the composition to Volt's bandana.
- Mood: intelligent, forward-looking, collaborative, and trustworthy.
- Composition: the title is the strongest focal point; the researcher and
  agent network form the secondary focal point; the technical motifs create a
  loose ring and lead the eye through the research workflow.
- Use layered depth and a dark blue-to-teal technical background, but keep the
  central title area quiet and high contrast.
- Ensure important elements stay inside safe margins for social-media crops.

## Avoid

- Do not use the generic words “Intelligent Textbook.”
- Do not include brand names, product logos, company marks, or recognizable
  commercial AI interfaces.
- Do not render paragraphs, labels, pseudo-code, equations, or illegible UI
  text; only the exact book title may appear as readable text.
- Avoid generic stock-photo clichés, photorealistic faces, handshakes,
  floating lightbulbs, humanoid robots, and disembodied hands.
- Do not imply that agents work without human supervision or verification.
- Do not let montage elements compete with or overlap the title.
