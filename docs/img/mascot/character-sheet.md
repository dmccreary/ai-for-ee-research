# Character Sheet: Volt the Border Collie

The canonical identity document for Volt, the pedagogical
mascot for the **AI for EE Research** textbook. Every pose prompt and every
piece of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to
the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Volt
- **Species:** Border Collie
- **Subject:** AI agents for electrical engineering research
- **Catchphrase:** "Trust, but verify!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Black and white border-collie coat — black back, ears and
  tail; white blaze, chest, muzzle and paws — hex `#1F1F1F` / `#FFFFFF`
- **Accent color:** University of Minnesota maroon and gold — hex `#7A0019`
  (maroon) and `#FFCC33` (gold)
- **Clothing / accessories:** A maroon bandana with a small gold
  lightning-bolt emblem, and a small gold whistle on a cord around the neck
- **Expression:** Alert, friendly, slightly wry smile; bright amber eyes and
  attentive ears (one ear semi-folded)
- **Size proportion:** Compact and full-bodied, readable at icon size (about
  90 px)
- **Art style:** Flat vector cartoon, clean lines, solid colors

## Personality

- Patient — never rushes a researcher past a step they have not understood
- Precise — cares about exact claims, units, and sources
- Curious — asks what the output means and how it could be checked
- Dry-witted — the occasional understated joke, never at the reader's expense

## Voice

- Short, direct sentences; one idea per sentence
- Frames agents as a flock to be directed and checked, never as colleagues
  who can be trusted blindly
- Often ends with a concrete question: "How would you check that?"
- Signature phrases: "Trust, but verify!", "Who checked the output?",
  "Round up the evidence."

## Pronouns

Volt has no gender. Always write "Volt" or *they/them*.

## Why This Mascot

A border collie is the breed best known for directing a flock with attention
and judgment rather than force, which mirrors the book's central idea: the
researcher orchestrates roughly 20 planning agents and 10 execution agents and
stays accountable for the result. The name *Volt* is an electrical-engineering
nod that is short, gender-neutral, and easy to say. The maroon bandana and gold
accents tie the character to the site's color palette.
