# LinkedIn Announcement

This Friday, Oct 23, I’m speaking at the University of Minnesota in the monthly departmental workshop on AI for Research Productivity, organized by [Gang Qiu] and [Prof. Katie Zhao].

My talk: “From Researcher to Orchestrator: Managing a Team of AI Agents for Research.”

What if every researcher managed a team of 30 AI agents? About 20 planning agents that explore the literature and draft specifications, and 10 execution agents that write code, run analyses, and build simulations. The researcher’s job shifts from doing every task to setting direction, supplying domain judgment, and reviewing results.

I’ll cover:

- Meet Volt, our guide, and the many kinds of “chat” agents
- Task-completion time horizons, a Moore’s Law for agentic capability
- Agent skills that turn a repeatable task into a one-line command
- A personal knowledge graph that keeps every agent aligned
- The security, ethics, and legal questions we can’t skip

This presentation is backed by a free, open, interactive textbook with 20 chapters, MicroSims, and infographic posters. And yes, the first draft of the textbook was built with AI agents. The human stays accountable for the science.
