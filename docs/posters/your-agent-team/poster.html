Meet Your Agent Team: Seven Specialists, One Chief of Staff

Each agent has a color, a role, and a task list. Ada coordinates the rest, and you approve what leaves the building.

Ada
Chief of Staff
Your assistant and coordinator of all six agents
  • Links papers, people, and notes into your knowledge graph
  • Triages email and drafts replies in your voice
  • Guards your calendar and flags conflicts
  • Hands work to the other six agents and merges results
  • Writes your morning briefing
Scout
Novelty and Prior-Art Search
Is this idea really new?
  • Searches papers, patents, and code
  • Reports the closest prior art, with a link
  • Watches your topics and sends a weekly digest
  • Finds missing citations before you submit
  • Logs every query so it can be rerun
Marisol
Events and Speakers
From invitation to confirmed seminar
  • Runs the department seminar and event calendar
  • Proposes, invites, and confirms speakers
  • Books rooms, catering, and a host
  • Collects titles, abstracts, bios, and A/V needs
  • Sends reminders and thank-you notes
Penny
Lab Budgets and Finance
Every penny accounted for
  • Tracks grant spending line by line
  • Forecasts burn rate and flags overruns
  • Drafts purchase requisitions for your signature
  • Prepares travel expense reports
  • Builds proposal budgets with Rosa
Rosa
Grant Writing
Funding calls to finished proposals
  • Finds funding calls that fit your research
  • Builds the outline and compliance checklist
  • Drafts aims, approach, and broader impacts
  • Plays skeptical reviewer on your draft
  • Tracks internal and sponsor deadlines
Pixel
Presentations and Web
Clear slides, current web page
  • Builds slide decks and speaker notes from a paper
  • Redraws figures in one consistent style
  • Lays out conference posters
  • Keeps your university web page current
  • Checks every slide number against its source
Kai
Conferences and Proposals
Deadlines met, talks accepted
  • Tracks calls for papers and deadlines
  • Vets venues and flags predatory ones
  • Drafts abstracts and talk proposals
  • Tracks submissions, reviews, and rebuttals
  • Plans registration and travel
