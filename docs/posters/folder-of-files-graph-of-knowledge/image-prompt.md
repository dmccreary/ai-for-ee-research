# A Folder of Files, a Graph of Knowledge — Image Generation Prompt

Please generate a new image.

## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind.** No title, no headings, no labels, no letters, no numerals, no captions, no punctuation marks used as symbols (do not draw a question mark or an exclamation mark), no logos, no watermark, no readable code, no readable equations, and no legible screens. Search boxes are empty rounded bars with a magnifier icon. Documents, notebook pages, meeting notes, spines, and the bibliography show only unreadable abstract marks: blurred gray lines, color blocks, and generic waveform curves with no axis labels or tick numbers. All names, descriptions, and the title are added later by an interactive HTML overlay driven by a separate data file. If any lettering or numbers appear anywhere in the picture, regenerate.

## Image Specifications

- **Format:** PNG
- **Dimensions:** 1920 × 1080 px, landscape, 16:9 ratio. If the model cannot produce this exact size, generate at any 16:9 size, because the overlay uses percentages.
- **Background:** Warm off-white (#FAF7F2) with a very faint blueprint grid
- **Audience:** Graduate students, postdocs, and faculty in electrical and computer engineering
- **Style:** High-quality, polished illustration with soft lighting from the upper left, rich detail, and a consistent line weight. Semi-realistic is welcome: a real-looking cluttered desk, a corkboard, printed papers, a lab notebook. Saturated but professional colors. No glowing brains, no chrome robot hands, no stock-photo clichés. People are faceless or softly stylized. Agents are small friendly teal (#1F7A8C) workers or neutral machine icons, never humanoid robots with faces.
- **Palette:** maroon #7A0019 (the human researcher and accountability), gold #FFCC33 (focal points and the highlighted path), teal #1F7A8C (agents and automation), signal green #3C8D5A (verified, grounded), brick red #C0392B (risk), charcoal #2A2E3A (structure), off-white #FAF7F2 (background). Never use color alone: pair each with an icon or pattern.

## What to Draw

One poster split into a **left half and a right half**, made of **seven clearly separated panels**. The left half is the naive version, a folder of files. The right half is the controlled version, the same material as a graph of knowledge. **Use the same camera angle, the same scale, the same objects, and the same background on both sides**, so that a student compares the two halves at a glance. Every panel is a rounded rectangle with a thin colored border and a slightly lighter off-white fill, separated from its neighbors by a clean gutter of at least 0.8% of the image, and about 3% down the middle between the two halves. Nothing may cross a panel border. Keep a 2% outer margin. Do not add a title bar, header, footer, legend, or side panel: the panels are the whole composition.

The left half has three panels stacked in one column. The right half has two wide panels stacked on top, and below them two smaller panels side by side. The recurring story is one set of research items: a paper, an experiment, a dataset, a design, a concept, and a person. On the left they are loose objects. On the right they are typed nodes.

## Layout Notes
