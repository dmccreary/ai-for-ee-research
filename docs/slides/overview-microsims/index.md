## About This Deck

- **One slide at a time:** the [slide viewer](../slide-viewer.html?input=overview-microsims/slides.md) renders only the current slide, so a MicroSim loads when you reach its slide and is unloaded when you leave it.
- **Source:** the slides are plain markdown in `slides.md`, separated by `---` lines.
- **Keys:** arrow keys, Page Up/Down, or Space to move; `C` for the contents list; `F` for fullscreen.
- **Click a simulation, then use the buttons:** after you click inside an embedded MicroSim, the arrow keys go to the simulation, so use the Prev and Next buttons or click the slide margin to hand the keys back.
