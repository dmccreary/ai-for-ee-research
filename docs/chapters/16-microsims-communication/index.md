# MicroSims and Research Communication

## Summary

This chapter covers the ways research results are communicated and taught: slide decks, interactive simulations, MicroSims, embedded simulations, learning graphs, and intelligent textbooks. It introduces generating a MicroSim with an agent. After this chapter you can create an interactive simulation that illustrates a concept from your research.
