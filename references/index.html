    References¶

    This page collects some of the general references for this interactive intelligent textbook. Each chapter also includes a detailed list of annotated references you can use to explore more about that chapter's topic.

    A Semantic Web Architecture for Advocate Agents to Determine Preferences and Facilitate Decision Making¶

    1. Ketter, W., Batchu, A., Berosik, G., and McCreary, D. (2008). A semantic web architecture for advocate agents to determine preferences and facilitate decision making. In Proceedings of the 10th International Conference on Electronic Commerce (ICEC '08), Innsbruck, Austria. ACM, New York, NY, USA, pp. 1-10. DOI: 10.1145/1409540.1409554.
